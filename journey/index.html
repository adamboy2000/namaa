Our journey

Built over time.
Built for what’s next.

From the first facility in Los Angeles to a growing network across the Middle East. The milestones behind NAMAA.

2016

First facility

CSS opens its first facility in Los Angeles, California, focused on delivery restaurants.

2018

Leadership & investment

Travis Kalanick acquires a controlling stake in CSS, attracting significant investment including from Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund in January 2019.

2019

Middle East entry

CSS enters the Middle East with its first United Arab Emirates subsidiary and acquires the Motor City 1 facility in Dubai. It later expands to Saudi Arabia and Kuwait with sites in Riyadh and Kuwait City.

2020

KitchenPark launch

Operations commence under the KitchenPark brand, with delivery-kitchen sites going live in the United Arab Emirates and Kuwait.

2021

Regional expansion

Rapid regional expansion across Riyadh, Jeddah, the Eastern Province, Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Kuwait City, supported by local real estate and technology investments.

2022

New market: Bahrain

Launch of the first ground-up development in Dubai Silicon Oasis and entry into Bahrain with a property in Umm Al Hassam.

2023

Bespoke infrastructure

Launch of the Bespoke Infrastructure business with a purpose-built project for a meal plan provider in Saudi Arabia, alongside growing demand for specialised infrastructure.

2024

Diversified growth

Expansion of kitchen infrastructure across dozens of urban Middle East locations. Entry into quick-commerce infrastructure with new partnerships and specialised hubs.

2025

Scale milestones

The group surpasses 1,200 occupied kitchens. Its quick-commerce footprint reaches 24, with more than 10 bespoke infrastructure projects completed or in development. Partnerships are established with Delivery Hero, Keeta, Jahez, Amazon and Noon.

2026

A fifth market. A new chapter.

Our first facility launches in Qatar, our fifth Middle East market, as the group reaches more than 2,000 built kitchens. NAMAA introduces Picnic and Future Foods to the region.
